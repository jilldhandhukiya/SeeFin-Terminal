# SEEFIN TERMINAL

> **Next-Generation Financial Intelligence, Multi-Asset Blotter & Quantitative Risk Cockpit**

SeeFin Terminal (SeeTrade) is a premier institutional financial workstation designed for portfolio managers, proprietary trading desks, family offices, and quant analysts. Built with an uncompromising focus on ergonomics, speed, and real-time execution clarity, SeeFin delivers the speed and analytical depth of legacy $25,000/year institutional consoles through a modern, cloud-independent interface.

---

## Executive Summary

Modern financial markets demand split-second decision making, cross-asset risk visibility, and zero latency. SeeFin bridges the gap between fragmented web dashboards and cumbersome legacy terminals by consolidating market monitoring, options analytics, dual-entry accounting, private wire feeds, and paper trading into a unified, high-density workstation.

- **Zero Data Leakage**: Sandboxed client architecture where portfolio blotters, credentials, and trades remain private.
- **Unified Cross-Asset Coverage**: Equities, Derivatives (F&O), Commodities, Currencies, and Digital Assets under one command plane.
- **Tactical Bloomberg Ergonomics**: Hairline-grid tiling, amber-phosphor typography, customizable CRT scanline raster modes, and function-key navigation (`F1`–`F6`).

## Core Product Desks & Features

### 1. Market Monitor & Benchmark Ticker (`F1`)
- **Global Index Multi-Tape**: Instant visibility across NSE, BSE, NASDAQ, S&P 500, and global macroeconomic bellwethers.
- **Dynamic Micro-Sparklines**: Real-time intraday price trajectories and tick-by-tick momentum tracking.
- **Integrated Economic Calendar**: High-impact central bank announcements, CPI prints, and sovereign yield shifts.
- **Live News Wire**: Real-time ticker tape and financial news headlines.

### 2. Dual-Ledger Treasury Desk (`F2`)
- **Institutional Capital Allocation**: Operational debit and credit tracking with automated transaction hashing (`TRX-KEY`).
- **Category Segregation**: Dedicated allocations for Logistics, Housing, Services, Executive Retainers, and Protection.
- **Instant Reconciliation**: Real-time audit trail and running balance calculations to keep private office cash flow synchronized.

### 3. Portfolio Management & Quantitative Risk Desk (`F3`)
- **Asset Allocation Visualizer**: Interactive SVG donut chart showing real-time distribution across Equities, Commodities, FX, Digital Assets, and Cash.
- **Multi-Asset Holdings Matrix**: Comprehensive asset blotter displaying unit quantities, unit cost, live valuation, allocation percentages, and 24-hour P&L.
- **Risk Analytics Engine**:
  - **Volatility Index & Liquidity Scoring**: Live exposure dials to prevent margin and liquidity traps.
  - **Quantitative Metrics**: Instant computation of Sharpe Ratio, 95% Daily Value-at-Risk (VaR), and Benchmark Beta.
  - **Advisor Rebalance Assistant**: Real-time drift detection with automated target rebalancing recommendations.
- **Quick Action Operations**: One-click Deposit, Withdrawal, Asset Rebalancing, and Comprehensive Valuation Reporting.

### 4. Real-Time Equity Screener (`F4`)
- **Multi-Factor Scanning**: Filter thousands of liquid instruments by Most Active, Top Gainers, Top Losers, and High Volatility.
- **GICS Sector Classification**: Isolate capital movements across Technology, Finance, Energy, Healthcare, and Consumer sectors.
- **Market Breadth Telemetry**: Live Advance/Decline ratios and trading volume indicators.

### 5. Encrypted Private Wire (`F5`)
- **Confidential Intelligence Stream**: Private institutional wire for offshore trust audits, capital calls, and real-estate acquisitions.
- **Categorized Priority Tagging**: High/Medium/Low priority flags for Compliance, Finance, Asset Closings, and Executive Decisions.
- **Searchable Intelligence Vault**: Instant keyword filtering across historical wire dispatches.

### 6. Paper Trading & Derivatives Lab (`F6`)
- **Execution blotter**:
  - Live tick quotes, full Level-2 orderbook depth, and recent print executions.
  - Market and Limit order ticket routing with simulated slippage and fill logic.
  - Position blotter tracking live unrealized P&L, entry prices, and one-click square-off.
- **F&O Derivatives & Options Chain**:
  - Strike ladders with Call/Put open interest (OI), volume, and implied volatility (IV).
  - Real-time Greeks calculation: Delta, Gamma, Theta, and Vega.
  - Put-Call Ratio (PCR) tracking, Max Pain calculation, and key support/resistance zones.

### 7. Customization & Display Suite
- **Aesthetic Modes**: Seamless switching between sleek Amber Modern, 1980s Retro Green Phosphor, and CRT Scanline Overlay.
- **High-Fidelity Telemetry**: 60 FPS hardware rendering with zero frame drop during rapid market updates.
- **Blotter Purge & Memory Management**: One-click local cache reset for clean daily simulation trading sessions.
